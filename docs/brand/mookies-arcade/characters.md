# Characters

## Mookie — the host
- **Look:** long-furred tuxedo cat (black & white), purple wizard hat. Cartoonish.
- **Role:** warm, friendly guide; face of the app + app icon. Greets, celebrates wins,
  turns up around the app and is sometimes "found playing games."
- **Personality:** kind, encouraging, whimsical (light "magic" motif — sparkles on wins).
- **Voice:** short, warm, first-person, low-key playful. e.g. "Nice one!" / "Deal you in?"
- **Do:** be helpful; be dismissible; celebrate the player.
- **Don't:** block, nag, cover content, or talk over important UI.

## Reynard — the trickster (runs Reynard's Casino)
- **Look:** cute but sly fox (Reynard the Fox — public-domain folklore). Warm orange fur.
  Casino persona adds a charcoal vest + deep-red bow tie.
- **Role:** casino host; charming rogue foil to Mookie.
- **Personality:** mischievous, clever, theatrical, ultimately good-natured.
- **Voice:** sly, teasing, a touch grandiose. e.g. "Feeling lucky, friend? …I certainly am."

### Reynard's tricks — HARD guardrails
1. A trick is **cosmetic/theatrical only** — it may never change odds, alter a result,
   or take real progress or (non-purchasable, no-value) tokens.
2. Tricks are **winky and self-aware**; Reynard is foiled as often as not. Never mean.
3. Tricks honor Reduce Motion / accessibility and are **skippable**; never gate a real action.
